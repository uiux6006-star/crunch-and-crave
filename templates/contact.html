Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in about {{ site.delivery_time }}. During peak hours it can take a little longer — you can follow your order live on the tracking page.

Is delivery free?

Delivery is free on orders above Rs. 1,500. Below that, a flat Rs. 200 delivery fee applies.

How can I pay?

Cash on delivery is available right now. Online payments (JazzCash, EasyPaisa and cards) are coming soon.

Do you cater for events?

Yes! Send us a message above with your date, headcount and favourite items and we'll put together a custom package.

Can I customise my order?

Absolutely — add any request (extra spicy, no onions, sauce on the side) in the order notes at checkout.

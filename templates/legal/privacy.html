{{ site.name }} ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery address, optional email, order notes and the items you order.
  • Payment references: if you pay by JazzCash, EasyPaisa or bank transfer, the transaction ID you give us. We never ask for or store card numbers, PINs or passwords.
  • Messages and reviews: anything you send through the contact form or review form.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps your cart working, and your IP address, used only to block spam.

How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and confirm your order, and to contact you about it by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email.
  • To verify online payments.
  • To reply to your messages and publish reviews you submit (only your name and review text are shown).
  • To protect the website from spam and abuse.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Sharing

Your name, phone and address are shared only with the rider delivering your order. We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for accounting purposes. You can ask us to delete your personal details at any time, unless we must keep them by law.

Cookies

We use one essential session cookie for your cart and a security cookie (CSRF). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us.
